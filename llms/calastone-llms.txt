# Calastone

> Calastone operates the world's largest global funds network, connecting asset managers, distributors, custodians and platforms to automate and digitalize the investment funds marketplace. Founded in 2007 and headquartered in London, it handles order routing, settlements, share-class conversions, dividends, transfers, reporting, money market services and tokenized fund distribution for 4,500+ clients across 58 countries, processing over £300 billion of investment value each month. Calastone is a wholly owned subsidiary of SS&C Technologies Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: SSNC).
